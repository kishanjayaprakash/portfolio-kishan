ProTTS: Prominence-Aware Malayalam Text-to-Speech

Language Technologies Research Centre (LTRC), IIIT Hyderabad

Research Objective

Natural speech depends on emphasizing the appropriate words to convey accurate meaning and intent. Existing neural TTS models often ignore word-level prominence, or require expensive, manually annotated stress labels that do not exist for low-resource languages such as Malayalam.

Technical Architecture

1. Forced Alignment: Aligned phonemes and audio using Montreal Forced Aligner (MFA) across 7 hours of female-speaker audio from the AI4Bharat IndicTTS dataset.
2. FastSpeech 2 Baseline: Trained a baseline acoustic model, then initialized ProTTS with a clustering head utilizing K-means-initialized stress centroids to detect stressed words automatically.
3. Emphasis Predictors: Trained duration, energy, and pitch predictors to synthesize emphasized words naturally without requiring manual labels during inference.

Note on Code Availability: This work is part of ongoing, unpublished research at IIIT Hyderabad. Source code and research artifacts are proprietary and cannot be shared publicly at this time.
